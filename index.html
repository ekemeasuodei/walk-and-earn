Sponsor programme

A page for businesses interested in sponsoring walking challenges. This form is a local preview and does not send information.

How sponsor funding should work

  • Sign an agreement specifying campaign dates, eligible countries and budget.
  • Record the sponsor's confirmed payment and reserve the reward portion.
  • Set a capped reward formula and maximum liability.
  • Report campaign performance transparently.

Draft reward rules

Use this as a starting outline; finalize it with legal and payment-provider advice before launch.

  • Eligible activity: steps recorded through supported devices, subject to fraud checks.
  • Reward formula: publish the exact rate, daily cap, campaign limits, and expiry rules before users participate.
  • Approval: steps and rewards remain pending until validation and sponsor budget checks pass.
  • Withdrawal: minimum ₦1,000 for eligible Nigerian users, only when the available funded balance is sufficient.
  • Countries: international availability depends on local law, sponsor coverage, identity checks, and payout provider support.
  • No guaranteed income: users must not be promised a fixed amount per step unless the funds are reserved and payment terms are enforceable.
Do not publish an unverified cash rate. A rewards ledger must distinguish pending, approved, reserved, paid, failed, and reversed entries.
